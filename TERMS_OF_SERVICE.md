# FamilyGuard Terms of Service

**Effective date:** 22 November 2026

## 1. Service
FamilyGuard is a family-safety utility for account access, opt-in location sharing, device check-ins and family pairing. It is not an emergency-response, medical or law-enforcement service.

## 2. Consent and lawful use
Use family and location features only with the knowledge and explicit permission of every participating device user and any additional consent required by applicable law. Do not use FamilyGuard for covert surveillance, harassment or unauthorized monitoring. Every user can stop sharing and revoke consent.

## 3. Location and device status
Location is sent only after the device user grants foreground location permission, enables consent and starts the visible location foreground service. An ongoing notification indicates sharing is active. Battery percentage is optional device telemetry. "Online" is an estimate derived from recent check-in time and may be inaccurate or delayed by network conditions, Android power management or revoked permissions.

## 4. Accounts and pairing
Users must protect their sign-in details. Family invite codes are short-lived, one-use codes. Do not share them publicly. Family creation requires a verified active Premium entitlement; joining an existing family requires a valid invite code.

## 5. Premium subscription
Where Razorpay recurring checkout is configured, Premium is offered at ₹999 per month in INR. The recurring price, renewal terms and payment methods available to a customer are displayed by the payment provider during checkout. Subscription activation depends on successful server-side signature and status verification or a valid provider webhook. A test, pending or failed payment does not unlock Premium. Future renewals must be cancelled through the available provider subscription controls or by contacting support. Refund requests are handled under the published Refund & Cancellation Policy and applicable law.

## 6. Acceptable use and limitations
Do not attempt to access another person's account or location without permission. Do not rely on FamilyGuard as the sole means of emergency communication or safety. The service may be unavailable during hosting, network or maintenance interruptions.

## 7. Data and deletion
See the [Privacy Policy](https://github.com/princeofworld/PrinceHacker/blob/main/PRIVACY_POLICY.md). Account-data deletion does not automatically cancel a Razorpay subscription or remove records retained by the payment provider. Users must separately cancel recurring payments.

## 8. Contact
For installation, account, privacy or billing support, use https://github.com/princeofworld/PrinceHacker/issues. Never post credentials, payment secrets, OTPs or precise location details publicly.
