# FamilyGuard Terms of Service

**Effective date:** 21 November 2026

## 1. Scope
FamilyGuard is an informational, consent-first family-safety utility. It is not an emergency-response service, medical service, or substitute for direct contact with local emergency services.

## 2. Consent and lawful use
Use location and app-usage features only with the knowledge and permission of the person using the device and any additional consent required by applicable law. Do not use FamilyGuard for covert surveillance, harassment, or unauthorized monitoring. Permission can be declined or revoked in Android Settings.

## 3. Current feature limitations
The current app build provides a user-initiated foreground location display, a user-reviewed SOS message handoff to the device messaging app, and an optional on-device app-usage summary after separate Android Usage Access approval and Premium entitlement. It does not provide continuous live location sharing or a persistent background tracking service. It does not provide continuous/background location tracking, location history, geofencing, remote device control, call recording, screen recording, private-message reading, or cloud family accounts.

## 4. Subscription
The intended Premium subscription product ID is `familyguard_premium_999_monthly`. The displayed ₹999 monthly amount is indicative only. The actual price, billing period, renewal, trial, tax, cancellation, and eligibility are those shown by Google Play at checkout. Google Play manages payment and subscription cancellation. Premium features are available only when Google Play and the configured FamilyGuard verification backend confirm an eligible active subscription. If the backend is not configured or cannot be reached, Premium stays locked.

## 5. Privacy and data deletion
The app's current version stores a local consent preference and does not operate a FamilyGuard cloud account. The in-app Account / Data Deletion action clears local FamilyGuard preferences. Google Play billing records are managed separately by Google. See [Privacy Policy](https://github.com/princeofworld/PrinceHacker/blob/main/PRIVACY_POLICY.md).

## 6. Availability and disclaimer
Features depend on device support, Android permissions, Google Play services, and network availability. Location estimates can be inaccurate or unavailable. SOS messages are not sent automatically; the user reviews and sends them in their messaging app.

## 7. Changes and contact
These terms must be revised when the app adds new data processing, accounts, background services, or subscription functionality. For questions, use the project's [GitHub issue page](https://github.com/princeofworld/PrinceHacker/issues). Do not post sensitive personal data, location details, passwords, or purchase tokens publicly.
