# PrinceCircle Terms of Service

**Effective date:** 27 November 2026

## 1. Service
PrinceCircle provides account access, opt-in family location sharing, device summaries, user-started camera/screen sharing, private photo uploads and device safety notifications. It is not an emergency-response, medical or law-enforcement service.

## 2. Consent and lawful use
Use family and location features only with the knowledge and explicit permission of every participating device user and any additional consent required by applicable law. Camera and screen sharing require a separate in-app disclosure and Android permission/system confirmation. Do not use PrinceCircle for covert surveillance, harassment or unauthorized monitoring. Every user can stop sharing and revoke consent.

## 3. Location and device summaries
Location is sent only after the device user grants foreground location permission, joins a family, enables consent and starts the visible location foreground service. An ongoing notification remains visible. Each location session automatically ends after 10 minutes and requires a fresh Start action. The app does not request background location permission. Optional summaries require separate user switches and Android permissions. They may include installed app names/package names/icons, aggregate screen time, call count/duration, contacts count and battery percentage. Call participants, phone numbers, contact names/content and SMS messages are not uploaded.

## 4. Camera and screen sharing
The user must start sharing from the Android app and accept its disclosure. Camera sharing requires camera permission; screen sharing requires Android's MediaProjection confirmation. A persistent notification with a Stop control remains visible. Video is transmitted using WebRTC to the signed-in account dashboard; the service does not record the live stream through its signaling API. Sessions expire after 10 minutes. The app does not request microphone permission.

## 5. Remote safety commands
The dashboard may send an alert or ring-style notification to a device registered to the same account. Delivery checks occur while the app is open in the foreground and are not guaranteed when the app is closed or background-restricted. Notification permission/settings must allow alerts. A temporary in-app pause request requires the device user's acceptance and can be declined or unlocked locally. It does not lock Android or other apps.

## 6. Accounts and pairing
Users must protect their sign-in details. Family invite codes are short-lived, one-use codes. Do not share them publicly. Family creation and Premium eligibility are governed by the current plan and account status.

## 7. Premium payment
Direct UPI Premium is a one-time ₹999 payment for 30 days. It does not automatically renew. Users submit a 12-digit UTR and access remains pending until an administrator verifies the matching credit in the bank's official transaction history. A UTR, screenshot or forwarded SMS alone is not proof of payment. Premium is not activated by an unverified client-side claim. Never send UPI PINs, OTPs, passwords or full bank messages to support.

## 8. Acceptable use and limitations
Do not attempt to access another person's account, camera, screen or location without permission. Do not rely on PrinceCircle as the sole means of emergency communication or safety. The service may be unavailable during hosting, network or maintenance interruptions. Android power management and notification settings can delay or prevent remote alerts.

## 9. Data and deletion
See the [PrinceCircle Privacy Policy](https://github.com/princeofworld/PrinceHacker/blob/main/PRIVACY_POLICY.md). Account deletion removes account-associated records as described in that policy. Direct UPI payments are one-time and do not create a recurring mandate. Records held independently by a UPI app or bank remain subject to that provider's retention practices.

## 10. Contact
For installation, account, privacy or billing support, use https://github.com/princeofworld/PrinceHacker/issues. Never post credentials, payment secrets, OTPs or precise location details publicly.
