# FamilyGuard Terms of Service

**Effective date:** 22 November 2026

## 1. Service
FamilyGuard is a family-safety utility for account access, opt-in location sharing, device check-ins and family pairing. It is not an emergency-response, medical or law-enforcement service.

## 2. Consent and lawful use
Use family and location features only with the knowledge and explicit permission of every participating device user and any additional consent required by applicable law. Do not use FamilyGuard for covert surveillance, harassment or unauthorized monitoring. Every user can stop sharing and revoke consent.

## 3. Location and device status
Location is sent only after the device user grants foreground location permission, joins a family, enables consent and starts the visible location foreground service while the app is open. While sharing is active, the service may request location updates approximately every 60 seconds, including while the app is minimized, and sends coordinates, accuracy, capture time and optional battery percentage to the opted-in family over HTTPS. An ongoing notification remains visible. Each session automatically ends after 10 minutes, stops location updates and turns off sharing consent. A new session requires the device user to explicitly press Start again. Users can stop sharing or revoke consent at any time. FamilyGuard does not request ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION. "Online" is an estimate derived from recent check-in time and may be inaccurate or delayed by network conditions, Android power management or revoked permissions.

## 4. Accounts and pairing
Users must protect their sign-in details. Family invite codes are short-lived, one-use codes. Do not share them publicly. Family creation requires a verified active Premium entitlement; joining an existing family requires a valid invite code.

## 5. Premium subscription
Direct UPI Premium is a one-time ₹999 payment for 30 days of access. It does not automatically renew; users must make a new payment each period. Users submit a 12-digit UTR and access remains pending until an administrator verifies the matching ₹999 credit in the bank's official transaction history. A UTR, screenshot or forwarded SMS alone is not proof of payment. Premium is never activated by an unverified client-side claim. Direct UPI payments do not renew automatically, so there is no recurring mandate to cancel. For a payment that is duplicated, incorrect, or not reflected in the account after verification, contact support through the project support page. Refund requests are reviewed under applicable consumer-protection law; this document does not promise an automatic refund.

## 6. Acceptable use and limitations
Do not attempt to access another person's account or location without permission. Do not rely on FamilyGuard as the sole means of emergency communication or safety. The service may be unavailable during hosting, network or maintenance interruptions.

## 7. Data and deletion
See the [Privacy Policy](https://github.com/princeofworld/PrinceHacker/blob/main/PRIVACY_POLICY.md). Account deletion removes the account and its associated FamilyGuard records. Direct UPI payments are one-time and do not create a recurring mandate. Records held independently by a UPI app or bank remain subject to that provider's retention practices.

## 8. Contact
For installation, account, privacy or billing support, use https://github.com/princeofworld/PrinceHacker/issues. Never post credentials, payment secrets, OTPs or precise location details publicly.


## Direct UPI configuration and admin review
The service may display a UPI QR code and payment intent for a UPI ID configured by the service administrator. The app does not read bank SMS or automatically approve payments from forwarded messages. Users submit a 12-digit UTR; an administrator must verify the exact credit in the bank app or statement before enabling Premium for 30 days. Never send UPI PINs, OTPs, passwords or full bank SMS messages to support.
