Privacy Policy

FamilyGuard processes the email address and password hash needed for account access. Passwords are not stored as plain text. Location, battery and check-in timestamps are received only while a member has explicitly enabled sharing and the visible Android service is active. Only opted-in member records are displayed to family members. Location records older than ten minutes are excluded from the dashboard. Data is transmitted over HTTPS. Revoke location consent in the app and request account deletion through the service. Payment details are handled by the payment provider; FamilyGuard does not store full card or UPI credentials.

Terms of Service

Use FamilyGuard only where permitted by law and with informed consent from each person whose device or location is shared. The app is a family safety aid, not an emergency service or guaranteed real-time tracker. Reports can be delayed by network conditions, Android battery controls or disabled permissions. Protect your account and pairing codes. Covert monitoring, harassment and unauthorized tracking are prohibited.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Premium is ₹999 per month where Razorpay recurring checkout is enabled. Checkout must display the recurring amount and renewal terms before confirmation. Cancel future renewals through the subscription controls provided by the payment provider or contact support. Cancellation prevents future scheduled charges but does not automatically reverse a completed billing cycle. Refund requests are reviewed under applicable consumer law and payment-provider rules. Report duplicate or failed charges with the transaction reference; never send passwords, card details, UPI PINs or OTPs. If payment configuration is unavailable, no payment is collected.

Contact Support

For installation, login, privacy or billing issues, use the official project support channel. Do not include passwords, location coordinates, payment secrets or personal documents.
